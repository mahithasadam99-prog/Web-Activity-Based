Food Categories

Spinach, carrots, broccoli and other vegetables.

Oats, brown rice, whole wheat and millets.

🥜 Protein Foods

Healthy Recipes

🥣 Vegetable Oats

Ingredients:

Oats, carrots, peas, onion and water.

Preparation: Cook oats with vegetables and water until soft.

Calories: About 250 kcal per serving.

🥗 Fresh Fruit Salad

Ingredients:

Apple, banana, orange and grapes.

Preparation: Wash, cut and mix the fruits.

Calories: About 150 kcal per serving.

🥗 Sprout Salad

Ingredients:

Sprouts, cucumber, tomato and lemon juice.

Preparation: Mix the ingredients and add lemon juice.

Calories: About 180 kcal per serving.

Daily Meal Plan

🌅 Breakfast

Idli, vegetable upma or oats with fruit.

☀️ Lunch

Rice or roti, dal, vegetables and curd.

🌇 Evening Snack

Seasonal fruit, nuts or roasted chickpeas.

🌙 Dinner

Roti, vegetables and a protein-rich food.

Healthy Eating Tips

✔ Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

✔ Include protein-rich foods in your meals.

✔ Choose whole grains regularly.

✔ Drink enough water throughout the day.

✔ Limit excess salt, added sugar and highly processed foods.

✔ Enjoy balanced meals and eat regularly.
